# RapidCCNA

A study game for the CCNA 200-301 exam. Learners work through units in blueprint order, with spaced repetition bringing back what they've studied.

## Progress

**Unit**:
One lesson and everything attached to it (tutorial, drills, labs, its own questions). It's the step the learner moves forward by.
_Avoid_: Topic, module, chapter

**Completed**:
A unit whose study steps the learner has finished once, all the way through, from either the daily session or free training. Completing a unit says it has been covered, not that the learner knows it.
_Avoid_: Done, finished, passed

**Mastered**:
A unit where the learner scores at least 85% over their last 20 unguided attempts or quiz answers. It measures skill and is separate from Completed.
_Avoid_: Completed, learned

**Daily session**:
The session the dashboard plans: due reviews, due drill refreshers, then the next unit that isn't Completed.
_Avoid_: Daily plan, lesson

**Free training**:
Studying any unit, quiz or lab picked from Topics, outside the daily session's order.
_Avoid_: Practice mode, on-demand

## Lessons

**Card**:
One screen of a lesson's tutorial, with a heading, a body and optionally an example.

**Info-dump card**:
A card that's mostly arbitrary values to memorise (numbers, orderings, lists) that can't be worked out from first principles, such as port numbers or syslog severities.
_Avoid_: Reference card, table card

**Memory aid**:
A mnemonic on an info-dump card that gives the values a pattern, a story or a hook so they stick, and points out values that are easy to mix up.
_Avoid_: Tip, hint (a hint belongs to guided drills)

## Questions

**Choice**:
One of the options in a multiple-choice question. Choices appear in a new random order each time the question is shown. Number keys pick by on-screen position.
_Avoid_: Answer (the answer is the correct choice or choices), option
